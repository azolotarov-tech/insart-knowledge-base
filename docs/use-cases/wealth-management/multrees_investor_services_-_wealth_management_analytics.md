# Multrees Investor Services — Automated Onboarding & Wealth Management Analytics

> **One-liner summary** — We designed and implemented an automated client onboarding pipeline and internal KPI reporting dashboards for a prominent UK wealth management service provider, eliminating manual document redundancy and optimizing treasury workflows.

---

## Problem Overview

Multrees Investor Services provides comprehensive outsourced investment services to wealth managers, private banks, family offices, and financial advisory businesses across the UK. The platform enables advisors to monitor, manipulate, and report on onshore and offshore assets denominated in multiple currencies, structuring investment routes based on specific end-investor goals (such as retirement or education savings).

Before INSART's engagement, the process of onboarding new client accounts and tracking advisory requests involved extensive friction. Advisory firms had to manually duplicate information across multiple systems. Specifically, after completing initial request flows, advisors had to fill out an entirely separate Microsoft Word document with the exact same data to generate the final Client Asset Form (CAF). Furthermore, internal management lacked centralized visibility into operational turnaround metrics, making it difficult to effectively track and enforce organizational KPIs.

---

## Challenges Identified

| # | Challenge | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Manual Data Duplication** | Wealth advisors were forced to manually transcribe matching transaction datasets into standalone Word files during onboarding. |
| 2 | **Opaque Operational Metrics** | Internal treasury and operational management lacked automated statistics to track how long it took teams to process incoming request forms and deliver client feedback. |
| 3 | **Complex Multi-Asset Tracking** | The platform needed to securely isolate, aggregate, and present mixed offshore/onshore multi-currency assets within localized reporting interfaces. |
| 4 | **Strict Enterprise Security Limits** | Due to stringent compliance mandates, development had to be executed entirely within highly restricted virtual machine (VM) environments. |

---

## Proposed Solution

INSART embedded a Senior .NET backend specialist to work directly within the Multrees core technology team. Operating entirely over secure virtual machines, we re-engineered the user onboarding data pathways and internal operational interfaces:

* **Automated Client Asset Form (CAF):** Converted the offline, manual text-duplication steps into a fully online, autogenerated digital form that pulls previously inputted parameters instantly.
* **Pre-Advice Validation Flow:** Built a unified database aggregation tier to channel adviser inputs directly to the Multrees internal treasury desk, coupled with automated email communication handshakes.
* **Management & KPI Dashboards:** Developed dedicated data views to provide managers with a clear overview of submitted requests and processing timelines.

### Key Users & Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| **Financial Advisors / Wealth Managers** | Submit investment requests detailing incomes, currencies, and asset structures while utilizing digitized onboarding forms. |
| **Multrees Treasury Desk** | Review aggregated investor profiles to evaluate financial data, perform liquidity checks, and authorize or decline incoming funds. |
| **Multrees Operations Managers** | Monitor request queues, audit internal turnaround speeds, and manage organizational performance metrics. |

---

## Process / Solution Flow

The automated data aggregation and client onboarding journey functions through four primary stages:

1. **Pre-Advice Request Intake** — The wealth advisor fills out the digital Pre-Advice Request Form outlining income sources, fund amounts, target currencies, and investment horizons.
2. **Treasury Desk Authorization** — The submitted data aggregates inside the secure database architecture, dynamically populating the Multrees Internal Form. The treasury team evaluates the portfolio profile and approves or rejects the fund acceptance path.
3. **Automated Notification & Confirmation** — Upon a treasury action, the system triggers automated email notifications to keep the advisor and internal desks aligned.
4. **Autogenerated Onboarding Execution** — The system transfers verified request parameters into the digital Client Onboarding CAF Form. This autogenerated online format eliminates the need for manual text transcription, allowing the advisor to instantly sign and submit the finalized data bundle.

## Business Outcome

* **Eliminated Onboarding Redundancy** — Replaced manual Microsoft Word transcriptions with an online, autogenerated client asset documentation engine, saving advisors considerable time and improving overall satisfaction.
* **Data-Driven KPI Optimization** — Launched a dedicated KPI analytics dashboard that monitors document processing speeds, enabling operations managers to measure turnaround times and address workflow bottlenecks.
* **Enhanced Treasury Oversight** — Delivered a secure, unified interface for the internal treasury desk, simplifying the process of auditing, verifying, and accepting multi-currency offshore and onshore assets.
* **Mockup-Driven UI Alignment** — Translated complex internal and external layouts into interactive React views, connecting backend relational database columns directly with targeted operational dashboards.

---

## Lessons Learned

* **Onboarding Automation as a Growth Driver** — Transitioning manual, document-heavy onboarding steps into auto-populating online workflows significantly reduces processing delays and accelerates capital time-to-market in wealth management systems.
* **Developing in Restricted Environments** — Developing on secure virtual machines requires close, proactive alignment with the client's internal IT operations to ensure steady delivery without compromising enterprise security protocols.
