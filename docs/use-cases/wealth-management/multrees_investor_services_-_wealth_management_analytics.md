# Multrees Investor Services — Automated Onboarding & Wealth Management Analytics

> **One-liner summary** — We designed and implemented an automated client onboarding pipeline and internal KPI reporting dashboards for a prominent UK wealth management service provider, eliminating manual document redundancy and optimizing treasury workflows[cite: 17].

---

## Problem Overview

Multrees Investor Services provides comprehensive outsourced investment services to wealth managers, private banks, family offices, and financial advisory businesses across the UK[cite: 17]. The platform enables advisors to monitor, manipulate, and report on onshore and offshore assets denominated in multiple currencies, structuring investment routes based on specific end-investor goals (such as retirement or education savings)[cite: 17].

Before INSART's engagement, the process of onboarding new client accounts and tracking advisory requests involved extensive friction[cite: 17]. Advisory firms had to manually duplicate information across multiple systems[cite: 17]. Specifically, after completing initial request flows, advisors had to fill out an entirely separate Microsoft Word document with the exact same data to generate the final Client Asset Form (CAF)[cite: 17]. Furthermore, internal management lacked centralized visibility into operational turnaround metrics, making it difficult to effectively track and enforce organizational KPIs[cite: 17].

---

## Challenges Identified

| # | Challenge | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Manual Data Duplication** | Wealth advisors were forced to manually transcribe matching transaction datasets into standalone Word files during onboarding[cite: 17]. |
| 2 | **Opaque Operational Metrics** | Internal treasury and operational management lacked automated statistics to track how long it took teams to process incoming request forms and deliver client feedback[cite: 17]. |
| 3 | **Complex Multi-Asset Tracking** | The platform needed to securely isolate, aggregate, and present mixed offshore/onshore multi-currency assets within localized reporting interfaces[cite: 17]. |
| 4 | **Strict Enterprise Security Limits** | Due to stringent compliance mandates, development had to be executed entirely within highly restricted virtual machine (VM) environments[cite: 17]. |

---

## Proposed Solution

INSART embedded a Senior .NET backend specialist to work directly within the Multrees core technology team[cite: 17]. Operating entirely over secure virtual machines, we re-engineered the user onboarding data pathways and internal operational interfaces[cite: 17]:

* **Automated Client Asset Form (CAF):** Converted the offline, manual text-duplication steps into a fully online, autogenerated digital form that pulls previously inputted parameters instantly[cite: 17].
* **Pre-Advice Validation Flow:** Built a unified database aggregation tier to channel adviser inputs directly to the Multrees internal treasury desk, coupled with automated email communication handshakes[cite: 17].
* **Management & KPI Dashboards:** Developed dedicated data views to provide managers with a clear overview of submitted requests and processing timelines[cite: 17].

---

## Process / Solution Flow

The automated data aggregation and client onboarding journey functions through four primary stages:

1. **Pre-Advice Request Intake** — The wealth advisor fills out the digital Pre-Advice Request Form outlining income sources, fund amounts, target currencies, and investment horizons[cite: 17].
2. **Treasury Desk Authorization** — The submitted data aggregates inside the secure database architecture, dynamically populating the Multrees Internal Form[cite: 17]. The treasury team evaluates the portfolio profile and approves or rejects the fund acceptance path[cite: 17].
3. **Automated Notification & Confirmation** — Upon a treasury action, the system triggers automated email notifications to keep the advisor and internal desks aligned[cite: 17].
4. **Autogenerated Onboarding Execution** — The system transfers verified request parameters into the digital Client Onboarding CAF Form[cite: 17]. This autogenerated online format eliminates the need for manual text transcription, allowing the advisor to instantly sign and submit the finalized data bundle[cite: 17].

## Business Outcome

* **Eliminated Onboarding Redundancy** — Replaced manual Microsoft Word transcriptions with an online, autogenerated client asset documentation engine, saving advisors considerable time and improving overall satisfaction[cite: 17].
* **Data-Driven KPI Optimization** — Launched a dedicated KPI analytics dashboard that monitors document processing speeds, enabling operations managers to measure turnaround times and address workflow bottlenecks[cite: 17].
* **Enhanced Treasury Oversight** — Delivered a secure, unified interface for the internal treasury desk, simplifying the process of auditing, verifying, and accepting multi-currency offshore and onshore assets[cite: 17].
* **Mockup-Driven UI Alignment** — Translated complex internal and external layouts into interactive React views, connecting backend relational database columns directly with targeted operational dashboards[cite: 17].

---

## Lessons Learned

* **Onboarding Automation as a Growth Driver** — Transitioning manual, document-heavy onboarding steps into auto-populating online workflows significantly reduces processing delays and accelerates capital time-to-market in wealth management systems[cite: 17].
* **Developing in Restricted Environments** — Developing on secure virtual machines requires close, proactive alignment with the client's internal IT operations to ensure steady delivery without compromising enterprise security protocols[cite: 17].
